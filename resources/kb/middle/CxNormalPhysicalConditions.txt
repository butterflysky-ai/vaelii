;; CxNormalPhysicalConditions — the states of matter of stuff at ordinary room temperature
;; and pressure. Sees the shared upper ontology via CxUniverse. CxWell does not see it,
;; so the everyday contexts below CxWell assume no temperature and serve a story set on
;; Mars as well as one set in a kitchen. A context opts in by placing itself under this
;; one, and a context under both CxWell and this one reads the everyday theories at room
;; temperature.
;;
;; CxAbstract places solid, liquid, gas and plasma under substance and separates them,
;; and says nothing about which substance is in which state. A substance's state depends
;; on its temperature and pressure, so the state belongs to a theory that fixes the
;; conditions. This theory fixes the ordinary ones: a room, a kitchen, a street on a mild
;; day. A theory of another temperature states its own states beside this one.

(genlCx CxNormalPhysicalConditions CxUniverse)

;; ---- the solids ---------------------------------------------------------
;; Stone, wood and glass are solid at room temperature. Glass is an amorphous solid: a
;; pane does not flow.
(genl stone solid)
(genl wood solid)
(genl glass_stuff solid)

;; ---- the liquid metal ---------------------------------------------------
(genl mercury liquid)

;; ---- a metal is solid, by default ---------------------------------------
;; A default rule with its exception rather than a genl edge per known metal: a metal
;; newly found, hypothesized or made up comes out solid unless the KB says otherwise.
;; Mercury is the stated exception. The rule concludes no solid membership for a portion
;; of mercury, so the portion's liquid membership meets no solid one and nothing clashes.
;; See docs/exceptions.md.
(exceptWhen (mercury ?x)
  (set/defaultRule (set/forwardRule (implies (and (metal ?x)) (solid ?x)))))
